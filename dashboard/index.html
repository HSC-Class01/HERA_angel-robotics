국내 Peer Firms

웨어러블 로봇 및 의료·재활 로봇 시장의 비교 후보입니다. 직접 비교 가능성과 최신 상장/공시 여부를 추가 검토해 주세요.

* EBITDA, net debt and borrowed-debt ratios are displayed only when relevant accounts can be mapped; EBITDA remains blank if only a partial depreciation/amortization account is found. DPO uses cost of sales as a proxy when credit purchases are not separately disclosed; CCC inherits that limitation. Half-year/quarterly profitability and turnover ratios are period-based, not annualized. The quick ratio shown as (current assets − inventory) / current liabilities is an approximation. ROIC uses EBIT after tax based on a disclosed effective tax rate and average (equity + interest-bearing debt − cash) invested capital; periods with unreliable/missing components are blank. Market-value ratios (PER, PBR, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield) are not calculated because share-price/market-capitalization data is not part of this OpenDART-only collector.
